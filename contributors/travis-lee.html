Travis Lee

A child of the nineties, Travis Lee currently lives in San Diego. His latest book is 'Abe's Decision' and previous publications include "Trust Me" in episode 19 season 22 of The NoSleep Podcast and "Covet Thy Brother's Truck" in the spring 2025 issue of The Writing Disorder. He's hard at work on a magical realism novel, and loves referring to himself in the third person.
