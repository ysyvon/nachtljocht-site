Christian Godwin

Christian Godwin is a PhD candidate and lecturer at the Ohio State University. His academic work combines realist and psychoanalytic theory to understand international politics. Before studying at OSU, Godwin earned an MA at the University of Chicago and worked as a case writer at Harvard Business School. His debut fiction story was published in The Bleeding Margin, and he is currently working on a book manuscript called Libidinal Power Politics.
