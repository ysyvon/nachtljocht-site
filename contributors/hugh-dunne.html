Hugh Dunne

Hugh Dunne is a retired software engineer and lifelong aspiring author. He finally became a published author when he was shortlisted for the 2026 Kate O’Riordan Prize for Short Fiction. His stories appear in First Light, the related anthology, and in the upcoming Hellbound Books Anthology of Folk Horror.
