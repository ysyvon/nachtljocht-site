A.J.L. Noda

A.J.L. is a humanities teacher interested in speculative fiction and the intersection of storytelling, economics, and ethics. He is an avid reader, writer, jazz guitarist and overall nerd. He has been dreaming of publishing a novel since he was just a grom. Oh, also he is currently obsessed with learning to surf. You can find more of his short fiction on Gavagai, Cryptic Frog Quarterly, Brian's Bookcase and thefreak.ca.
