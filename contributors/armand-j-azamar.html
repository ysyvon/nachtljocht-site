Armand J. Azamar

Armand J. Azamar is a speculative fiction writer from the Chicagoland area. He has written for several publications, including Epona Muse Publishing, Sinister Smile Press and Altered Reality Magazine. Azamar has also published two speculative fiction books: The Third Hour and Messengers from the Sky, both of which can be found on Amazon.
