Destyni Gessner

Destyni Gessner is a writer originally from Minnesota. Currently she is pursuing a master's in English from Duquesne University in Pennsylvania. Her work has previously been published in River Whale Review and performed at the Minnesota Shorts Festival and on KMSU. A bit of a cliché, she is fond of cats, books, and coffee.
