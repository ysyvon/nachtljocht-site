David Hartley

David Hartley is a writer of strange and unnerving short fiction based in the Peak District, UK. He is the author of Fauna (Fly on the Wall Press) & Incorcisms (Arachne Press), and runs creative writing workshops for The Dark Art of Storytelling. He can be found on Instagram at @DHartleyWriter.
