Louise Carey

Louise read English at University College, Oxford and has a master's in Psychology from Oxford Brookes. Her debut trilogy, Inscape, Outcast and Downfall, has been published in two languages, and the French edition of Inscape was shortlisted for the 2024 Prix Utopiales. Louise currently divides her time between writing and running an escape room company, which she founded with her partner and brother. When she's not designing puzzles or puzzling over plots, she enjoys reading.
